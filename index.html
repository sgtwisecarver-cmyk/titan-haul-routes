Haul Routes

Every approved haul route, every disposal, every safety document you need — right here, nothing to log into.

  • 🔍Search a pad and get the operator's approved route — turn-by-turn and the official map. That's the route the county agreed to, not the one GPS picks.
  • 📍Tap Nearest to sort every pad by distance from your truck.
  • 🚧Road alerts light up a pad's card when ODOT reports a closure or wreck on its route — checked every 10 minutes.
  • 🛡️Safety in the menu: customer SOPs, the site safety video, and Good Catch reporting.
