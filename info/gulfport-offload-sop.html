Gulfport — Reuse Offload SOP

All produced-water traffic arriving at Gulfport locations receiving reuse water must follow this SOP for a safe, efficient operation.

  1. All traffic into the Gulfport reuse location shall follow the Traffic Control Plan (TCP) for that location and obey all posted speed limits.
  2. Upon entering the authorized haul route, radio in your position on the designated CB channel (use the callout signs).
  3. When space is verified at the offload station on the pad, proceed into the location at a speed not exceeding 5 mph.
  4. Before backing into position, verify there are two spotters (one front, one rear) to assist safe movement.
  5. Once backed into the designated offload lane, set your brakes and await further instruction.
  6. The offload station attendant will review the JSA & truck manifest before you exit the vehicle.
  7. Place your fire extinguisher at the front of the truck, catch pan at your pump, and wheel chocks.
  8. The attendant places a large cone with a flag at the front of the truck marking that it is not to move. The cone and flag shall only be moved by the offload station attendant.
  9. Connect hose and ground to the truck.
  10. Once verified by the attendant and driver, open your valves and begin offloading. The driver is the only person qualified to operate valves on their truck.
  11. When offload is complete, disconnect the hose and verify all valves and caps on the truck are in proper position.
  12. Complete a 360° walk-around and remove your fire extinguisher / catch pan / wheel chocks. Do not get back in the truck to leave without first doing a 360 inspection.
  13. The attendant removes the flag once verified you're clear to pull away from the offload manifold and will escort the truck from its lane.
  14. Follow the TCP for exit from the Gulfport location.
