Site Safety — Production Site Elements

Know what you're standing next to. · ~12 min

Streams over data — about 43 MB if you watch the whole thing. On a weak signal, let it buffer a few seconds before you start.

Every production pad is built from the same handful of pieces — different operator, different paint, same jobs. You won't operate any of it. Knowing it is still the job, because the thing that hurts people on a pad is almost never the thing they were looking at — it's the thing they walked past.
