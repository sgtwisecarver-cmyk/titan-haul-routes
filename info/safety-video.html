Site Safety — Production Site Elements

Know what you're standing next to. · ~15 min

Streams over data — about 53 MB for the whole thing. On a weak signal let it buffer a few seconds first. Captions available — tap CC in the player to turn them on.

Every production pad is built from the same fifteen pieces — different operator, different paint, same fifteen jobs. Six of them you never touch — those belong to the operator. The other nine you work with — you load through them, ground to them, read them, and in an emergency use them. All fifteen you have to recognize, whatever name that pad calls them by, before you step out of the truck.
