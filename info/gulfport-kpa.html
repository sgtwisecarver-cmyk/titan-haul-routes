Gulfport KPA Reporting

See a hazard or need to report a Stop Work Authority on a Gulfport location? Use Gulfport's observation card — no account needed.

⚠️ Submit a Hazard Observation / Stop Work →
Scan to open the reporting form

How it works:

  1. Tap the button (or scan the QR code).
  2. It asks if you have a KPA account — if you don't, select "No".
  3. Fill out the observation form and submit.

This is Gulfport Energy's official external submission form for third-party contractors (Observation Card in KPA). For emergencies, always call dispatch first: (740) 433-5399.
