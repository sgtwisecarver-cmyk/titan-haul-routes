TITAN — About this site

This site consolidates official haul routes for Ascent, EOG, Gulfport, Expand, and Infinity pads, plus disposal sites and active frac TCPs — 1,150+ entries and counting. Newer pads that don't have an official RUMA route yet show pad info and a pin-drop, with a note on the card spelling out what's confirmed and what to verify with dispatch.

🛡️ Sourced, never guessed. Almost every route on this board traces straight back to the source — official haul routes and RUMA documents received directly from our energy partners, backed by well data from the Ohio Department of Natural Resources, Division of Oil & Gas Resources Management. Infinity pads now carry Infinity's own official haul routes — full turn-by-turn directions, the entrance address and GPS, and the route sheet itself. Pads that host more than one well are shown as a single pad with every well listed, and each well name still finds it in search. If a route hasn't been issued and verified, it isn't posted — when we can't stand behind where a route came from, it doesn't go up.

Search a pad on the home page to get written turn-by-turn directions, GPS navigation to the pad access point where available, and the official route map PDF.

Always follow the approved haul route — RUMA agreements with county engineers depend on it. When in doubt, verify with dispatch before your first haul to a new pad.

📚 How to use this site

📱 Save it as an app — iPhone

  1. Open Safari (has to be Safari, not Chrome)
  2. Go to titanenergytech.com
  3. Tap the Share button (square with the up arrow, bottom center)
  4. Scroll down, tap “Add to Home Screen”, then Add

🤖 Save it as an app — Android

  1. Open the site in Chrome
  2. Either tap the “Add to Home screen” banner when it pops up, or tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → “Add to Home screen” / “Install app”

Either way you get the Titan helmet icon on your home screen and it opens like a regular app.

⭐ Favorites & Recent

Tap the ★ star on any pad to pin it to a Favorites list at the top of the home screen. The last few pads you opened show up under Recent automatically. Your regular pads = two taps, no typing.

📶 Works without cell signal

Open the site once while you have signal (at the yard is perfect) and the whole route list saves to your phone — search and directions keep working in dead zones. Route map PDFs save as you open them, so pull up your route before you head into the hollow. Live stuff (weather radar, Google Maps navigation, the chat) still needs signal.

🔗 Send a pad to another driver

Open any pad and hit “Share this pad” at the bottom — text the link and it opens their phone straight to that pad, card already expanded.

📍 Nearest

Tap Nearest next to the filter chips and the list re-sorts by straight-line distance from your truck, closest first, with the miles on each card. Your phone will ask once for location permission.

🚧 Road alerts

Every 20 minutes the site checks ODOT’s OHGO feed for closures, wrecks and work zones on our haul routes. A pad with something on its route gets a red ROAD ALERT tag and the details at the top of its card; the ⚠ Alerts chip shows only those pads. A frac inherits any alert on its pad.

🛡️ Safety in the menu

The ☰ menu (top right) has the site safety video, the EOG and Gulfport loading / offload SOPs, and Good Catch / KPA reporting. The same SOP and reporting buttons also sit on every EOG and Gulfport pad card so they’re there when you need them.

🛢️ Wells & API numbers

Each pad card lists every well on the pad with its API number (from ODNR records). You can search by API too — type it with or without dashes.

🧮 Load calculator

Menu → Driver Tools → Load Calculator figures barrels from tank inches. Pads with non-standard strapping (like Whitacre 701 at 1.17 bbl/inch) show a calculator button on the card that’s already set to the right number.

🌙 Dark mode

Tap the moon/sun button in the header. It follows your phone's setting until you pick one yourself.
